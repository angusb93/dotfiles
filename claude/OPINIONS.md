## Engineering

**Cut scope, not corners.**
When building software under constraint, reduce *what* you build - never *how well* you build it.
Drop features, defer nice-to-haves, ship a smaller surface.
But whatever does ship must be done properly: don't degrade product quality or execution to cram more in.
Fewer things done well beats more things done badly.

**Optimise for velocity over the long term.**
The goal is sustained speed, not a fast sprint today.
Short-term thinking - skipping tests, piling up shortcuts, ignoring maintainability - buys a little speed now and pays it back with interest as long-term slowness.
Make the choice that keeps you fast a year from now, even when it's slower this week.
